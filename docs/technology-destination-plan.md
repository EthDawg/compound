# Technology destination — September 2026

The product question: help someone move from a company decision to the people,
markets and technical constraints behind it, without losing context.

## Required outcome

- Refresh every existing Workday and ServiceNow ANZ practice; separate local
  delivery evidence, published offers and credentials. Audit the global Workday
  roster and broad SI discovery entries. Add materially missing firms.
- Add concise ecosystem direction and practical buyer questions, grounded in
  acquisitions, people movement and capability evidence.
- Add a Global View: geographic context, market groupings, dependencies and
  source-backed implications. Show selected clusters rather than fake rankings.
- Explain technology history and conditional futures: Turing, universality,
  AI winters and the complementary changes behind apparent sudden breakthroughs.
- Make scale tangible: compute/memory/network orders of magnitude, latency vs
  throughput, LEO/MEO/GEO motion and propagation. Label models and assumptions.
- Link directly to useful satellite, spacecraft, launch, flight, cable and
  infrastructure explorers, explaining what their displays actually represent.
- Help CIOs and other buyers reason about invention, adoption, complements,
  migration and reversibility. Interactive examples are models, not forecasts.
- Keep Compound’s navigation and branding distinct from company studies.
- Update the public project, validate the implementation and deploy the same
  hosted edition. Record source dates and verification limits.
